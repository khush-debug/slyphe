The Wardrobe

Form, without fuss.

Celestia is building a point of view around refined silhouettes and the confidence of a well-chosen piece. Every garment is constructed with soft floating canvassing, natural shoulder rolls, and generous interior room that drapes effortlessly in motion.

Atelier Specification

100% Biella milled virgin wool woven with 15% mulberry silk. Breathable all-season weight (320gsm). Hand-stitched horn buttons, cupro sleeve lining, and double-pleated relaxed trousers.

Made to become yours.

We believe the strongest pieces are the ones you return to, then make your own. Tailored without rigidity, allowing natural wear to enhance rather than diminish the character of the cloth.
